# 📊 Himachal Tourism — Interactive Power BI Dashboard

**Project 4 of my Business Analytics journey**

This is my first interactive **Power BI** dashboard. I used real government
tourism data for **Himachal Pradesh, India** to build a multi-visual,
interactive dashboard that explores tourism trends over time, by district, and
by month.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To turn raw tourism data into an interactive dashboard that answers three
questions at a glance: **When** do tourists come? **Where** do they go? And
**who** are they (domestic vs foreign)?

---

## 📂 The Data

**Source:** Himachal Pradesh Tourism Department (2008–2025).
The dataset (included as `himachal_tourism_data.xlsx`) has three tables:
- **Yearly Trend** — total arrivals per year, 2008–2025
- **District 2025** — arrivals by all 12 districts
- **Monthly 2025** — arrivals by month

*Total tourists analysed: over 1.45 crore (domestic + foreign).*

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Get Data, building visuals, formatting, slicers
- **Line charts** — for trends over time
- **Bar / column charts** — for comparing districts
- **KPI Card** — for the headline total
- **Slicer** — an interactive Year filter
- **Data storytelling** — choosing the right chart for each question

---

## 📈 The Dashboard — What It Shows

The dashboard contains six connected visuals:

1. **Line chart — Yearly arrivals (2008–2025):** shows steady growth to a 2017
   peak, the **81% COVID crash in 2020**, and a fast recovery.
2. **Bar chart — Foreign tourists by district:** **Kangra leads (30K)**, thanks
   to Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj.
3. **Bar chart — Total arrivals by district:** **Kullu and Shimla** dominate.
4. **Line chart — Monthly arrivals:** strong seasonality, **June is the peak**,
   September the quietest.
5. **KPI Card — Total tourists:** ~259M across the full period.
6. **Year slicer:** an interactive filter to view specific years.

---

## 💡 Key Insight

The districts that attract **foreign** tourists are **not** the same as those
that attract **domestic** crowds. Kullu and Shimla pull the biggest *total*
numbers, but **Kangra** attracts the most *foreign* visitors because of its
Tibetan-culture destinations (Dharamshala, McLeod Ganj). This is a useful
signal for targeted, segment-specific tourism marketing.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Plan for seasonality** — scale staff and services up for the April–June
   and December peaks, and promote the quiet months (e.g. September).
2. **Market by segment** — promote Kangra and culture-rich districts to foreign
   travellers; promote hill-station districts to domestic travellers.
3. **Build crisis resilience** — the 2020 crash showed how fast a shock can hit;
   flexible planning protects the sector.
